Programs · Class 6–8

One throughline. Three grade tracks.

Each year adds a layer: circuits and mechanisms in Class 6, code and control in Class 7, connected systems and a capstone in Class 8. Schools can run one track or all three.

Plan your rollout See a term inside
Grade tracks & formats

Pick a track. Or run the whole school.

The three grade tracks form a continuous journey. The whole-school formats sit alongside them for assemblies, clubs, teacher training and innovation weeks.

Class 6

Spark Starters

First circuits, first mechanisms, first “it works!” moments. Students learn to use tools safely and explain how a build behaves.

  • LEDs, motors, switches and simple sensors
  • Cardboard mechanisms and wheeled chassis
  • Block-based coding and debugging habits
  • Mini showcase at the end of term
Bring Spark Starters to your school
Class 7

Circuit Coders

Students move from block coding to text, wire their own sensor circuits and build a robot that reacts to its environment.

  • Arduino programming and serial debugging
  • Ultrasonic, IR and light sensors
  • Line-following and obstacle challenges
  • Build log and peer demo
Bring Circuit Coders to your school
Class 8

IoT Inventors

The capstone year. Students connect devices to a dashboard, work with live data and present a solution to a real school problem.

  • ESP32, Wi-Fi and cloud dashboards
  • Data logging, charts and simple analytics
  • Capstone project with a real user
  • Public showcase and completion certificate
Bring IoT Inventors to your school
Whole school

Innovation Day

A one-day design sprint for mixed grades. Teams get a real brief, a kit and five hours to build something worth showing.

  • Assembly kick-off and team formation
  • Facilitated build sprint with mentors
  • Parent open lab and judging
  • Certificates and photo story

Teachers

Teacher Studio

A five-day studio for the teachers who will run the lab. Less theory, more hands on the same kits their students will use.

  • Kit fluency and classroom safety
  • Lesson planning from the ready-made scope
  • Assessment, rubrics and evidence
  • Monthly clinics for the first year

After school

RoboQuest Club

A 90-minute weekly club for students who want more lab time. Open projects, competition prep and a lot of iteration.

  • Open build time with mentor support
  • Competition and exhibition preparation
  • Student-led workshops and peer teaching
  • Termly showcase to families

A term inside the programme

Fourteen weeks, three clear phases.

Every term has the same rhythm, so students and teachers always know what comes next. The projects change; the structure does not.

Weeks 1–4 · Launch

Tools, safety and first sparks.

Students learn the kit, the routines and the language of the lab. The first build is deliberately small so every team can finish and explain it.

  • Lab induction and component mapping
  • One working circuit per pair
  • Notebook setup and build-log habits
4 sessionsPairsBaseline assessment
Weeks 5–10 · Build

Iterate, debug, repeat.

The middle of the term is a build sprint. Teams choose a problem, sketch a solution and keep improving a prototype that does something real.

  • Problem brief and user interview
  • Weekly build review with a mentor
  • Debugging log and peer feedback
6 sessionsTeams of 3–4Mid-term check-in
Weeks 11–14 · Showcase

Finish, explain, celebrate.

Teams polish their build, prepare a short demo and present to an audience. The term ends with evidence — a working project and a student who can explain it.

  • Demo script and display board
  • Peer judging and visitor Q&A
  • Certificates and termly progress report

Rollout

Start small. Prove it. Then scale.

We recommend a one-term pilot with a single grade before rolling out to all three. It keeps the risk low and gives your teachers a real say.

01

Discovery call

A 30-minute conversation about your goals, space, budget and the teacher who will champion the lab.

02

Campus audit

We visit, measure and map the room. You receive a layout, equipment list and fixed quote.

03

Pilot term

One grade, one lab, one showcase. We co-teach and collect feedback from students and teachers.

04

Scale & sustain

Add grades or formats, train new teachers and move the lab into your annual academic plan.
