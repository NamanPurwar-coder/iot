Quick answers

Before you write to us.

Do you travel to our city?

We work with schools across India and confirm mentor coverage before quoting. For cities outside our regular routes, we can start with a teacher-training and lab-setup package, then add on-campus delivery once a cluster of schools is confirmed.

Can we start mid-term?

Yes. Many schools start with a six-week taster or a single Innovation Day before committing to a full term. We will map the start date to your calendar and exam weeks.

Who owns the equipment?

The school owns the kits and infrastructure we install. You receive a shared equipment register, and we handle replacements and consumables for the duration of the partnership.
