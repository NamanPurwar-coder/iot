The project journey

A whole project, from scribble to showcase.

Every build follows the same six-stage loop real engineers use. Kids never just follow instructions — they design, test and improve until it works.

01 / 06 Session 1 of 6

Start with a real problem

Every project opens with a question, not an instruction manual. Could a bin open on its own? How does a rover “see” a wall? Kids unbox the kit, meet the parts and choose a challenge worth solving.

  • Unbox and name every component
  • Play with demo circuits and sensors
  • Choose a problem they actually care about
Kids walk away with

A challenge, a safety habit and a kit they know their way around.
