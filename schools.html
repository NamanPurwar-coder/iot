The complete ecosystem

A lab is the easy part. The programme is the point.

Equipment alone does not change a classroom. We wrap every lab in the curriculum, training and routines that keep it useful after the novelty wears off.

01

Curriculum & lesson plans

A 36-week scope mapped to grade-level science and maths, with ready-to-teach slides, worksheets and project briefs.

02

Teacher enablement

A five-day onboarding, monthly clinics and co-teaching in the first term. Your teachers finish as lab leads, not spectators.

03

Kits, spares & safety

Lockable storage, labelled components and a low-voltage safety routine for every class. Consumables are replenished each term.

04

Assessment & certificates

Skill rubrics, termly progress reports and completion certificates — plus judging support for your showcase.

05

Events & innovation fair

An internal Demo Day each term and a place at our regional RoboQuest Innovation Fair, with judging and visitor tours.

06

Parent & community comms

Termly newsletters, open-lab afternoons and shareable project films that show families what the lab actually does.

Questions school leaders ask

Before you book a demo.

If your question is not here, ask us directly — we answer school enquiries within two working days.

Do we need a dedicated room?

A classroom works if it has power, Wi-Fi and lockable storage. We audit your space before quoting and can also run a shared lab on a trolley that moves between rooms.

Will our teachers need to become robotics experts?

No. We deliver the first term alongside your teachers, then co-teach and hand over. Training, lesson plans and a mentor helpline are included in every partnership.

What does a school partnership cost?

Pricing depends on the lab format, cohort size and whether you want us to run sessions or only set up and train your team. We send a written quote after the campus audit, with no per-child surprises.

Is it safe for Class 6 students?

Yes. We use low-voltage kits, insulated tools, supervised storage and a safety induction for every class. Soldering is introduced only in Class 8, with eye protection and ventilation.

Can it align with our board and timetable?

Modules map to CBSE and ICSE science and maths topics. We can deliver as a weekly period, an after-school club or a term-long intensive, and we share the mapping before you commit.

What happens after the first year?

You keep the curriculum and equipment register. We refresh projects, train new teachers and run the showcase. Schools can scale from one grade to all three without starting over.
